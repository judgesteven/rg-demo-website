Players earn good-cause points for using safer-play tools

An opt-in programme that rewards checking your play, setting limits and taking the self-assessment. Never spending.

Sample data throughout. Tap a mission in the app to see how it works.

  1. Missions point to tools you already havePlay history, limits and the self-assessment stay in the player's account. Short quizzes and stories live on the pilot page.
  2. Confirmed in real time through the APIWhen a player uses a tool, the account sends one event with a pseudonymised ID. Their mission updates in under a second.
  3. Rewards go to good causesPoints become donations in the player's name. No free plays, no bonus money, no ranking of individual players.
